truth and perspective -- art candidates

(1) when you cannot raise your head low

1A -- the empty stalk An empty rice stalk stands upright. Below it is a small lit stone it never bends to. (A full stalk bows. An empty one stands tall.)
1B -- two stalks The proverb drawn whole. The full stalk bows down to the ground, and the empty one keeps its head high.
1C -- moon beneath He stands chin up under an empty sky. The moon is only in the pool at his feet. This picks up "reflection" from the long version.

(2) truth warred love

2A -- blade and bloom A planted blade stands straight beside a wilted flower. The flower's head hangs, but it is still rooted.
2B -- still in the glass One wilted rose, still in its glass of water. Its head hangs over the rim and two petals lie on the sill. Nobody threw it out.
2C -- line and curve The barest version. A straight line, and a curve that bent against it and still stands.
