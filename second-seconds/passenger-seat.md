# passenger seat

passenger seat with my backpack  
and no hands to hold  
no dj to set the tune  
no companion to hold a conversation  
no one to kiss and no one to scratch  
no one to lick and no one to bite  
no one to annoy and no one to annoy  
no one to fixate on a random object  
and explain the beauty  
and no one to sit down next to  
or place my head onto

life is life  
and will continue to be  
but life is either a journey  
or an atonement  
or a mix of both

and now, it is a prolonged solitude  
with sudden intermissions
